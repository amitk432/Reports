## 🔬 3. Detailed Microscopic & Histopathological Findings

### A. Light Microscopy (LM) Examination
1. **Glomerular Architecture:**
   - Total of **13 glomeruli** sampled; **1 glomerulus** is globally sclerosed.
   - Viable glomeruli are markedly enlarged with prominent mesangial matrix expansion and mesangial hypercellularity.
   - Classical **Nodular Glomerulosclerosis (Kimmelstiel-Wilson lesions)** identified in multiple glomeruli.
   - Glomerular basement membranes (GBM) demonstrate diffuse thickening highlighted on Jones Silver Methenamine stain.
   - **No evidence** of proliferative crescents, endocapillary proliferation, necrosis, or fibrinoid necrosis.
2. **Tubulointerstitial Compartment:**
   - **50% to 60% interstitial fibrosis and tubular atrophy (IFTA)** with focal areas of "striped fibrosis".
   - Tubules show acute tubular injury manifested by epithelial flattening and brush border attenuation.
   - Interstitium shows diffuse chronic inflammatory infiltrate composed predominantly of lymphocytes and monocytes.
3. **Vascular Architecture:**
   - Afferent and efferent arterioles display prominent, circumferential **hyaline arteriosclerosis**.
   - Medium-sized arteries show marked fibroelastic intimal hyperplasia.

### B. Immunofluorescence (IF) Study
- Sample contained **4 viable glomeruli**.
- Direct immunofluorescence revealed:
  - **IgG:** Weak to moderate linear staining (**1-2+**) along the peripheral glomerular capillary basement membranes (typical diabetic pseudo-linear pattern).
  - **Kappa Light Chains:** Linear capillary wall staining (**1+**).
  - **Lambda Light Chains:** Linear capillary wall staining (**1-2+**).
  - **IgA, IgM, C3, C1q:** Negative (No immune-complex deposits identified).

## 🎯 4. Definitive Pathological Diagnosis

> **Impression:** **Class IV Diabetic Glomerulosclerosis (Diabetic Nephropathy)** with advanced chronicity (**50–60% interstitial fibrosis and tubular atrophy**) and marked arteriolosclerosis.

### Clinical Impact
This biopsy established that the patient's end-stage renal disease is irreversibly chronic, validating the permanent requirement for maintenance hemodialysis (MHD) and ruling out treatable crescentic or lupus-associated glomerulonephritis.
